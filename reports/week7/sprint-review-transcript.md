# Sprint 5 Review — Interview Guide & Transcript

## Interview Guide

> Use this section to prepare for and conduct the Sprint 5 customer review session.
> Questions marked **[REQUIRED]** must be covered — they are needed for the handover assessment.
> Questions marked **[FEATURE]** cover specific Sprint 5 deliverables.

---

### Section 1: Product Access & Independence [REQUIRED]

1. **[REQUIRED]** Can you access the portal right now at [su-portal](https://10.93.26.192/)? Does it load correctly on your device?
2. **[REQUIRED]** Have you or another SU member tried using the admin panel independently (without our help)? If yes — what did you try to do, and did it work?
3. **[REQUIRED]** Is there anything about the portal that you feel you cannot operate or maintain without us being around?

---

### Section 2: Handover Readiness [REQUIRED]

4. **[REQUIRED]** Do you know how to create and publish a new event through the admin panel?
5. **[REQUIRED]** Do you know how to create a new questionnaire (poll) and see the results?
6. **[REQUIRED]** If something breaks on the portal — do you know who to contact and what information to provide? (See [DEPLOY.md](https://github.com/plaksiki/SU-Website/blob/main/DEPLOY.md))
7. **[REQUIRED]** Are there any features you expected to have by now that are still missing?

---

### Section 3: Sprint 5 Feature Feedback [FEATURE]

8. **[FEATURE]** We separated the admin panel into distinct pages (events, polls, members). Does this layout feel more convenient than before?
9. **[FEATURE]** We added an **Edit** button for events — so you can update event details (title, time, location, description) after the event is created. Have you had a chance to try it?
10. **[FEATURE]** We added a **photo gallery section** to past events: you can now add photo URLs and a link to a Yandex Disk album in the event edit form, and they appear on the event page for visitors. Is this something you will actually use? How do you currently store and share event photos?
11. **[FEATURE]** Member photos on department pages now load faster (via image optimization). Have you noticed any change in loading speed?

---

### Section 4: Weekly Progress Check [FEATURE]

12. **[FEATURE]** This week Dasha implemented the **PUT /event/{id} endpoint** — the backend API for editing events. The frontend edit form is now connected to it. Were you aware of this, and does the edit flow work end-to-end from your perspective?
13. **[FEATURE]** Are there any data or fields you'd want to edit on an event that we haven't included yet? (Currently: title, description, date, location, finish date, photo URLs, gallery link.)

---

### Section 5: Satisfaction & Next Steps [REQUIRED]

14. **[REQUIRED]** On a scale of 1–5, how satisfied are you with the portal at this point?
15. **[REQUIRED]** After the course ends and the project is handed over — what are your plans for the portal? Will you keep it running? Will someone maintain it?
16. **[REQUIRED]** Is there anything you'd like us to document or explain before the final handover?
