## General Assessment

As first-year students with limited prior development experience, AI tools were used selectively to support debugging, documentation, implementation planning, and routine development tasks. The team remained responsible for all architectural decisions, code implementation, testing, and final design decisions. AI primarily served as an assistant for troubleshooting, documentation review, transcript processing, CSV export guidance, and implementation clarification, while all core development work and final solutions were completed and verified by the team.
