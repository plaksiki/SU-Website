# Sprint 2 Retrospective

## What Went Well

1. We successfully deployed MVP v1 to the university virtual machine(VM). The application is now publicly accessible via [a live URL](http://10.93.26.192) and auto-updates when the repo is updated.

2. We effectively connected backend, frontend and databases, therefore demonstrated a ready-made tab with questionnaires.

3. We successfully conducted an interview with the customer and found out our pros and cons in the work and the next instructions for the 3rd sprint, which we need to focus on.

4. The team stayed in touch every day via chat in Telegram, which allowed us to quickly share updates without waiting for formal meetings.

5. The team completed all planned sprint tasks on time. All assignments task was splited between members and completed in time too.

6. We were able to find the right time to attend the meeting for both clients.

## What Did Not Go Well

1. Many team members worked with tools for the first time (React,
TypeScript, microservices, backend), so this caused tasks to take
longer than expected. For example, it took a whole meeting for two of us to
discuss about how they will upload frontend and backend so it is
like one whole piece but there still two structures inside(backend
and frontend pieces of code).

2. Some tasks were postponed until the end of the sprint because some of the team members was unable to communicate every day, which created
unnecessary haste and pressure ahead of the deadline, as everything
must be documented in reports.

## What you changed compared to the previous Sprint

1. We clearly assigned all responsibilities to all team members at the beginning of the sprint. Certain issues were linked, where a team member understands and it is not very difficult for him, it belongs to his range of possibilities.

2. We tried to arrange an interview time with the customers in advance so that both customers could be present for a full and high-quality review.

## Process improvements for the next Sprint

1. Clearly allocate tasks at the beginning of each sprint and understand the burden on each team member, especially those who cannot attend the interview with the customer or are on some kind of trip to avoid
rushing at the last minute before the deadline.
