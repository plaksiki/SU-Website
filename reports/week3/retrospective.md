# Sprint 1 Retrospective

## What Went Well

1. We successfully deployed MVP v1 to a virtual machine. The application
is now publicly accessible via a live URL, which is a concrete and
visible result of the sprint.

2. The team stayed in touch every day via chat in Telegram, which allowed
us to quickly share updates without waiting for formal meetings.

3. The team completed all planned sprint tasks on time.

## What Did Not Go Well

1. We were unable to find a time that worked for both customers
to attend the meeting.

2. Many team members worked with tools for the first time (React,
TypeScript, microservices, backend), so this caused tasks to take
longer than expected.

3. Some tasks were left until the end of the sprint, which created
unnecessary rush and pressure close to the deadline.

## Action Points

1. Reach out to both customers at least 3 days in advance to agree
on a meeting time that works for everyone.

2. Distribute tasks clearly at the beginning of each sprint to avoid
last-minute rushes before the deadline.
