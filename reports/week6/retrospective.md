# Sprint 4 Retrospective

## What went well

1. In backend we implemented `GenerateValue` and a cleaner endpoint format 
   for frontend needs (#191); created PostgreSQL tables and GET/POST/DELETE 
   endpoints for events, connected to frontend (#196)
   
2. In frontend we added a bio card for each member that appears when clicking on them, and redesigned the questionnaire download panel.

3. The team stayed in touch every day via chat in Telegram, which allowed us to quickly share updates without waiting for formal meetings.

4. The team completed all planned sprint tasks on time. All assignments task was splited between members and completed in time too.

5. We were able to find the right time to attend the meeting for both clients.
   
## What did not go well
1. Since the meeting with the clients was at the end of the week, we didn't manage to finish redesigning the admin panel; however, as it wasn't urgent, we moved the task to the next sprint.
2. We realized that since the backend and frontend didn't work in coordination at the start, almost all of the site's logic was on the frontend.
## What the team changed or attempted to change based on the previous Sprint Retrospective, and what results they observed
Based on the previous retrospective, we decided to keep a single backend 
developer for now and put the dark mode idea on hold, since new design 
revisions had come up. This kept the team's capacity stable, and by the 
end of the sprint backend and frontend work was well-covered by the 
current team size, a single backend developer has been sufficient. The 
early-sprint coordination gap between backend and frontend also resolved 
itself naturally as the sprint progressed, without needing a separate fix.
